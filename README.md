ランサーとアーチャーの掛け合いを楽しみながら創作や作業を進める、Windows向けのファンメイドウィジェットです。今日の作業や原稿の進捗を記録し、集中して貯めたポイントで小物や衣装を解放できます。

「ロボットさんしゅごい♥」で終わらず、人の創作を実際に支えるAIと、そうしたAIとの共生を探求します。

**[配布ページ・使い方](https://changung-widget-20261111.sulino.chatgpt.site/?lang=ja)** · **[1.5.4の更新履歴と以前のバージョン](CHANGELOG.md)**

今日の作業カード、「少し進めた」、創作日誌・最近7日間、日々の作業量、集中タイマー、記録に応じた会話、小物とSDの一枚絵イベント、ルーンの助言、参考資料の本棚に対応しています。日本語・韓国語・英語を切り替えられます。記録はPCに保存され、会話は用意した台詞から選ばれます。外部AIへの問い合わせはありません。

## English

**For AI that makes human effort shine brighter, rather than diminishing its worth.**

YARIYUMI is a fan-made Windows desktop companion featuring Lancer and Archer. Keep today's tasks and creative progress close by, focus with a countdown timer, and earn points toward props and outfits.

We explore AI that offers practical support for human creativity, and ways to work alongside it beyond simply asking a robot to draw for us.

**[Download site and installation guide](https://changung-widget-20261111.sulino.chatgpt.site/?lang=en)** · **[1.5.4 changelog and older versions](CHANGELOG.md)**

Version 1.5.4 focuses on character fidelity across everyday, work, touch, prop and rune dialogue. Archer keeps his measured statements and dry retorts, while Lancer retains his seasoned, easy manner. Korean, Japanese and English wording has been aligned. Three short conversations were added, and Archer's face and glasses now stay aligned when he turns his head with his hair swept up.

Version 1.5.3 adds right-click shortcuts to [Seta Maker](https://setamaker.netlify.app/) and [YARIYUMI · Fate/Lost Log](https://lancerarcher.vercel.app/). Both games are also linked in the download page’s top and bottom menus.

Version 1.5.2 lets you save images, PDF and HTML files in Reference shelf and link them to manuscripts or work. It adds reduced image previews, HTML text reading, opening original PDFs, and original-file export. Search titles, original filenames, HTML text, tags and notes; importing the same file again preserves its organization, notes and work links.

Existing features include dialogue memory across restarts, a resume card for recent journal steps or unfinished work, a brief visual afterglow, and quick actions. One customizable global shortcut is available and is OFF by default.

Includes partly-done markers, a dated creative journal, seven-day review, recurring project presets, daily progress logs, contextual dialogue, props, static SD cutscenes, rune advice and a reference shelf. Korean, Japanese and English are supported. Records stay on your PC, and dialogue uses locally selected authored lines without external AI calls.

Made with help from Codex. Character interpretations, dialogue and relationship portrayals are based on **behelit's fan works**.
